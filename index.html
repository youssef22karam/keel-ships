    Drag to orbit, scroll to get closer, or inspect the continuous hull in wireframe. The underwater bow is part of the hull, not an attached sphere.

    Enter the bridge. Drag to look and use WASD to walk. Click the radar to change range, the wheel to steer, or the throttles to adjust the simulated instruments. The aft door opens as you approach.
